Pastor David TeminiJesu

Pastor David TeminiJesu

Pastor David is called to raise men. He believes strongly that spiritual virtues should influence physical values, meaning believers are not meant to be aloof from the world but use their God given knowledge and prowess to influence it. He teaches the word of God with simplicity, clarity and humor and is often known by this phrase, ‘de with me’. He has a strong prophetic edge which has been a blessing to a lot of people across the world.

He’s a graduate of psychology, happily married to Pastor Debbie and the proud father of Daron and Temi. You can follow him on all social media platforms. Just search David TeminiJesu and you’re good to go.
